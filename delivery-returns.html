Delivery & Returns

Delivery Information

Order Processing

All orders are carefully packaged and processed within 1 to 2 business days (excluding weekends and public holidays) after receiving your order confirmation email.

Local Delivery (Dar es Salaam)

For orders within the city, we offer standard local courier delivery. Expected delivery time is typically same-day or next business day once the order is processed.

Upcountry Shipping

For deliveries to other regions, we partner with reliable logistics and bus courier services. Delivery times generally range from 2 to 4 business days depending on the destination.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order has been dispatched, we will send you a notification via WhatsApp or Email containing your courier details and instructions on how to collect or receive your package.

Returns & Exchange Policy

We want you to love your TALISM experience. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we are here to help.

Return Conditions

  • Timeframe: You have 7 days from the date of delivery to request an exchange or return.
  • Clothing & Handbags: Items must be unused, unwashed, and in the exact same condition that you received them. All original tags must still be securely attached.
  • Perfumes & Fragrances: For hygiene and safety reasons, perfumes cannot be returned or exchanged once the outer seal or packaging has been opened, unless the item arrived damaged.
  • Sale Items: Discounted items are considered final sale and cannot be returned.

How to Initiate a Return

To start a return, please contact our Customer Care team directly via our official WhatsApp at +255 684 143 537 with your order details and reason for return. If your return is accepted, we will provide instructions on how and where to send your package.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. Approved refunds will be processed back to your original method of payment (e.g., mobile money transfer) within 3-5 business days.
