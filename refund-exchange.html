Refund & Exchange Policy

Our Commitment to You

At TALISM, we take pride in the quality of our stara clothing, premium handbags, and signature scents. We understand that sometimes an item may not be exactly what you expected, which is why we offer a straightforward refund and exchange process.

Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return or exchange, your item must meet the following criteria:

  • The return or exchange request must be initiated within 7 days of receiving your order.
  • Clothing & Handbags: Must be unworn, unwashed, and in the exact condition you received them, with all original tags and packaging intact.
  • Perfumes: Due to health, safety, and hygiene regulations, fragrances are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable once the outer seal or packaging has been tampered with or opened.
  • Proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt) is required for all transactions.

Non-Returnable Items

Please note that the following items cannot be returned or exchanged:

  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or purchased during promotional discount periods.
  • Gift cards.
  • Opened or used fragrances and beauty products.

The Refund Process

1. Inspection

Once your returned item is received at our facility, our quality control team will inspect it to ensure it meets our return conditions. We will send you a message to notify you that we have received your returned item and whether your refund has been approved.

2. Processing

If approved, your refund will be processed immediately. The funds will automatically be applied to your original method of payment (such as M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, or Bank Card).

3. Timeline

Please allow 3-5 business days for the refund to reflect in your mobile money or bank account, depending on your provider's processing times.

Exchanges

We only replace items if they are defective, damaged upon arrival, or if you require a different size in our stara clothing range. If you need to exchange an item for the same product in a different size, please reach out to us via WhatsApp.

Shipping Costs for Returns

You will be responsible for paying your own shipping or courier costs for returning your item, unless the item was received damaged or an incorrect item was delivered to you by mistake. Original delivery fees are non-refundable.

If you are receiving a refund, the cost of return shipping (if arranged by TALISM) will be deducted from your refund.
