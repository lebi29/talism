Product Care Guide

Expert tips to keep your TALISM fashion, perfumes, and accessories in flawless condition.

Stara Apparel Care

Washing Instructions

Hand-wash or use a delicate machine cycle with cold water. Avoid aggressive bleaching agents to protect fabric fibers.

Drying Guidance

Air-dry in a shaded, well-ventilated area. Avoid direct sunlight during drying to maintain vibrant color saturation.

Ironing & Steaming

Use a garment steamer or iron on low heat setting. Always iron delicate embroidered pieces inside-out.

Fabric Care Pro Tip

For garments with intricate lace or beadwork, place the item inside a soft mesh laundry bag before machine washing.

Signature Scents Care

Optimal Storage

Store perfumes in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, humidity, and heat sources to preserve top notes.

Proper Application

Apply to pulse points (wrists, neck, inner elbows) from 15cm away. Avoid rubbing wrists together after spraying.

Cap Tightness

Keep the bottle cap securely fastened when not in use to prevent gradual evaporation of natural fragrance oils.

Handbags & Accessories Care

Daily Storage

Store handbags in dust bags when not in use. Fill with tissue paper to maintain original structural shape.

Surface Cleaning

Wipe clean using a soft, slightly damp microfiber cloth. Do not soak leather or synthetic luxury finishes.

Hardware Care

Keep zippers, buckles, and metallic chains dry. Gently polish metallic hardware with a clean dry cloth.
