How to Take Your Measurements

For the most accurate fit, keep the measuring tape firm but not tight against your body.

1. Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping tape level under arms.

2. Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, typically the narrowest part of your torso.

3. Hips

Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips/seat.

4. Full Length

Measure straight down from the highest point of your shoulder down to the floor.
